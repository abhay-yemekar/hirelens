Glass-box AI resume screening: large-language-model scoring against **versioned rubrics**, with **per-criterion evidence spans** you can verify, a **hash-chained audit trail**, and **adverse-impact reporting**.

## Why glass-box

Most AI screeners hand you a number and ask for trust. HireLens hands you the reasoning: every score points at the exact quoted text in the resume that produced it, every override is recorded, and every decision requires a stated reason. The audit log is **hash-chained** — tampering breaks the chain visibly.

## The workflow

1. **Create a job** with its full job description.
2. **Derive a rubric** — the LLM proposes criteria from the JD; HireLens owns the shape: slugged keys, six anchored levels per criterion, normalized weights.
3. **Upload resumes** — text, markdown, or PDF; extraction, hashing, and per-job deduplication happen automatically.
4. **Score** — each candidate is scored per criterion with quoted evidence located at character offsets in the document text.
5. **Review** — blind-view masking hides identity cues while keeping evidence spans aligned; decisions require reasons.
6. **Audit** — one-click adverse-impact reporting with the four-fifths rule, plus an immutable audit log.
