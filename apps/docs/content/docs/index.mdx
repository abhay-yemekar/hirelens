CandidLens scores candidates against a **rubric you control**. Every criterion's score quotes the exact resume text behind it, every decision needs a stated reason and lands in a **hash-chained audit log**, and anyone with viewer access can run an **adverse-impact report**. It was called HireLens until November 2026.

## Why it works this way

Most screening tools hand you a number and ask for trust. CandidLens hands you the reasoning: each score points at the quoted text in the resume that produced it, at its character offset. When a quote can't be found in the resume, the score is withheld instead of guessed. Overrides are recorded, and tampering with the audit log breaks its chain visibly.

## The workflow

1. **Create a job** with its full job description.
2. **Derive a rubric.** The model proposes 5–8 criteria from the job description; CandidLens fixes their shape: named keys, an anchored 0–5 scale per criterion and weights that add up. You review and edit before anything is scored.
3. **Upload resumes:** PDF, Word, text or Markdown. Extraction, a format check, hashing and per-job deduplication happen automatically.
4. **Score.** Each candidate gets a score per criterion with quoted evidence located in the document text.
5. **Review.** Blind review hides identity cues while keeping the quotes aligned, and every decision requires a reason.
6. **Audit.** A one-click adverse-impact report applies the four-fifths rule, next to the tamper-evident audit log.
