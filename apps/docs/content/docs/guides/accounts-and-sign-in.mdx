title: Accounts and sign-in
description: Sign-in methods, passwords, email confirmation, changing your email and deleting your account.
---

Everything here lives under **Settings → Sign-in & security** and **Settings → Delete account**, for recruiters and candidates alike.

## Ways to sign in

- **Email and password.** Passwords are 8 to 128 characters, and every password field can show what you typed.
- **Google or GitHub**, when the server has their keys (see [configuration](/docs/reference/configuration#sign-in)).

One account can use several methods. Settings lists them, and you can:

- **Add a password** to an account made with Google or GitHub, so you can also sign in with your email. This needs a sign-in within the last 15 minutes.
- **Change your password**, optionally signing out every other device.
- **Remove your password**, or **connect and disconnect** Google and GitHub. The last way to sign in can't be removed.
- **Sign out other devices.**

Google or GitHub never joins an existing account on its own, even when the email matches: connect them from Settings while signed in. Otherwise someone could register a stranger's email first and share the account once its owner signed in with Google. The sign-in page explains this when it happens.

## Email features

These need the server to send email (see [configuration](/docs/reference/configuration#email)). Without email, the links below are hidden.

- **Forgot password.** The link works once and expires after 1 hour. Following it sets a new password, or the first one for an account made with Google or GitHub, and signs the account out on every device. It also marks the address as confirmed, since the link reached the inbox.
- **Email confirmation.** Sign-up sends a link to confirm the address; Settings shows whether it is confirmed and can send it again. Opening the link only confirms the address; it never signs anyone in. Signing in never waits for confirmation.
- **Change your email.** Needs a sign-in within the last 15 minutes. A single-use link (1 hour) goes to the new address; it opens a page, and the change applies only when you press Confirm while signed in to the same account. Confirming cancels any open password-reset link, and the old address gets a notice.

Each address receives at most 3 emails of each kind per hour, and the server sends a limited number per day, so nobody can flood an inbox or use up the sending quota.

## Deleting your account

**Settings → Delete account** shows what will happen before anything is deleted. It needs a sign-in within the last 15 minutes and your email typed back.

| Organization | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Only you are a member | Erased with everything in it: jobs, candidates, resumes and its audit log |
| Others are members and you are the only owner | Deletion is blocked until you make another member an owner (Settings → Members → Owner) |
| Anything else | You leave. Its data stays with the other members, its audit log records that you left, and API tokens you created there are revoked |

Your name, email, password, Google and GitHub links, sessions, memberships and pending invitations are removed, and your email is free for a new account. The account keeps only an anonymous random id, because each audit log entry's hash includes its author: removing the id would break the audit chain of every organization you worked in. Deletion is all or nothing, and it can't be undone.
