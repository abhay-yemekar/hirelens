---
title: Resume Doctor
description: A deterministic resume check for candidates, with every finding pinned to its line.
---

The [Resume Doctor](https://candidlens.vercel.app/doctor) checks a resume the way an applicant-tracking system reads it, and tells the candidate what to fix. It is the candidate-side counterpart to scoring: no job description and no model involved.

## What it checks

About 30 checks in six groups:

| Group | Looks for |
| --- | --- |
| Contact | A name, an email address and a phone number in plain text |
| Structure | Standard section headings, and roles the parser can read |
| Dates | Dates on the roles, in order, and none in the future |
| Impact | Numbers that show results, bullets that open with a concrete verb, no first person |
| Brevity | Overall length and overly long bullets |
| Parseability | Scanned pages, tables, multi-column layouts and broken encoding |

Each finding is pinned to the exact line of the resume, so the page can highlight it.

## How the score works

- The score starts at **100**, and each finding subtracts a fixed amount. Nothing is curved, and nothing can raise a weak resume's score.
- **Excellent** needs 85 or more and no hard failures. **Good** is 60 or more. Below 60, or with two or more hard failures, the band is **needs work** whatever the number.
- The checks are plain code, so the same text always gets the same report.
- "No email" or "no dates" are reported as facts about the text; the Doctor never invents content for a resume.

## What it reads

Paste text, or upload a PDF, Word (`.docx`), `.txt` or `.md` file of up to 10 MB. A scanned PDF has no selectable text; export a text-based PDF or Word file instead, or paste the text.

Role lines are read in the common forms, for example:

```text
Senior Engineer – Acme Corp – Jan 2022 – Present
Senior Engineer—Acme Corp, Remote (Pune, MH) Mar 2026 – Present
Senior Engineer at Acme Corp, 2021 – 2024
```

Dates can also sit on the line under the title. When an Experience heading is found but no role line can be read, the Doctor says so ("Couldn't read your roles") instead of claiming there is no experience section.

## Privacy

The Doctor needs a signed-in account. Candidates outside an organization get the report without anything being stored. Inside an organization, a score history is kept, storing a hash of the text, never the resume itself.
